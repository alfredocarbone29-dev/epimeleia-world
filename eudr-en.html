WHAT IT IS ABOUT

What the EUDR is

From December 2026, to sell certain products into the European Union you will have to prove they did not come from land deforested after 2020.

The EU Deforestation Regulation —the EUDR— covers soy, cattle, wood, coffee, cocoa, rubber and palm oil, and everything derived from them. If your product is on that list and goes to Europe, your buyer will ask for the proof. Without proof, it doesn't get in.

We use the same data logic as governments and organisations worldwide to look back at your land and tell you, precisely, whether there was deforestation —and in which year— from 2020 to today.

WHERE WE FIT

THE DDS · YOUR STATEMENT
The Due Diligence Statement
It is the document filed in the EU's TRACES system. It is prepared by the operator or exporter, with their own data, geolocation and risk assessment. That filing is yours or your operator's.
EPIMELEIA · YOUR EVIDENCE
The proof of no deforestation
It is the evidence your DDS rests on: satellite evidence, sealed on blockchain and verifiable by your buyer or auditor, that your land has been clean since 2020. Without relying on our word.

You —or your operator— file the statement. We give you the proof that backs it.
