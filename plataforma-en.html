WHAT SETS US APART

We don't sell the data. We sell the proof that no one touched it.

Anyone can get a satellite image. What you can't buy elsewhere is having that image recorded in a way that no one — not you, not us, not the satellite — can alter afterwards.

WHEN THERE IS AN OBSERVATION

The satellite sees, records, and the result is written to the blockchain. No one can change it afterwards — not the client, not us.

WHEN THERE ISN'T

If clouds or a failure block the view, that gap is written down too: we call it an opacity gap. It is not a penalty — it is the contract working.

Missing data is data too. And it cannot be hidden or invented either.

That is why, when it matters, no one will be able to dispute the facts: that the river changed course, that the land was occupied before or after, that the area was still intact. The proof remains, dated, and it does not depend on anyone confirming it.

WHAT IS AT STAKE

Today, in many places, proof is no longer optional

We didn't invent these problems. This is how the world works now — those already living it know what we mean.

EXPORTING
Selling to Europe without proof of origin
The European Union requires exporters of soy, beef, timber or coffee to show that their product does not come from deforested land. A signed statement is no longer enough: they ask for traceable evidence.
Without that proof, the goods don't get in.
FINANCING
Applying for a green loan
Banks and funds finance environmental projects, but they increasingly demand verifiable data that what they finance is real. A folder full of promises, with no traceable record, does not get past the credit committee.
With proof in hand, the conversation changes.
DEFENDING YOURSELF
Being accused, and unable to prove otherwise
Someone says you polluted a river, or occupied a piece of land, on a given date. Without a dated record that no one can forge, it is your word against theirs.
With the proof, there is nothing to argue.

None of this is a threat we bring. It is the ground you are already standing on. All we do is make sure you arrive with the proof, not empty-handed.
